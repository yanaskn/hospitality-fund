What we do

Two ways in. One standard: it has to work on the floor.

Build

We design, build and run software inside hospitality businesses we have access to. When it works for one operator, we spin it out for the rest, with that operator as the first client.

Event OS started this way.

Invest

We back early teams solving hospitality problems with technology. Capital, hands-on advisory from people who run venues, and a real floor to test on.

Famished and MEDam are backed this way.

About

Started inside the venues.

Running events for hundreds of guests a night exposes the gaps generic software leaves: seating, menus, vendors, guest screens, all held together by spreadsheets and people. We built the tools we needed, then saw other operators needed them too.

Along the way we met founders solving adjacent problems and backed the ones who understood the floor as well as the code. Hospitality Fund exists to take ideas from a working operation to a product other operators will pay for.
