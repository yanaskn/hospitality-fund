What we do

Two ways in. One standard: it has to work on the floor.

Build

We design, build and run software inside hospitality businesses we have access to. When it works for one operator, we spin it out for the rest, with that operator as the first client.

Event Portal and Vendor OS started this way.

Invest

We back early teams solving hospitality problems with technology. Capital, hands-on advisory from people who run venues, and a real floor to test on.

We have invested in all three ventures below.

BuildSmart operations

Event Portal

Host and venue operations

One portal where the host builds the menu from the venue's real packages, manages the guest list and seating, and sees the placecard screens update at the ballroom doors. Sales managers see every change live. Built with L.A. Banquets across two venues and spinning out as a standalone product.

ProofIn production at two venues, every event week
BuildMarketplaces

Vendor OS

Venue vendor network

Lets event venues run their preferred-vendor network as a business: hosts book curated vendors through the venue, every referral is attributed to the event, and payouts split between vendor, venue and platform on one rail. Starting with AV and lighting at L.A. Banquets.

StatusIn build; first vendor mixer at The Landmark, January 2027

About

Started inside the venues.

Running events for hundreds of guests a night exposes the gaps generic software leaves: seating, menus, vendors, guest screens, all held together by spreadsheets and people. We built the tools we needed, then saw other operators needed them too.

Along the way we met founders solving adjacent problems and backed the ones who understood the floor as well as the code. Hospitality Fund exists to take ideas from a working operation to a product other operators will pay for.
